MWM Common Issues (field experience):
- Throttle sticking: alarm G197.3, check bearings/cavity/linkage. Clean or replace.
- Cylinder temp high: open air valve or reduce gas mixer. Temp low: opposite.
- Load fluctuation: fouled speed sensor or turbo surging. Clean sensor, install bypass.
- Start failure: check compressed air 6-8 bar, solenoid valve, starter pinion, spark plugs.
